As business and brand owners, we all strive to create that first "wow moment" for our customers: an unboxing experience that blows their mind and hooks them for life.

- For an aquarium, it's the amazement on a kid's face when they see an octopus up close for the first time.
- For a local plant nursery, it's the joyful anticipation a person feels as a flower bud slowly blooms.
- For a toothpaste company, it's the pride a kid feels when they brush on their own for the first time.

What's that first magical moment that **YOU** strive to create for your customers? And how do you help them visualize it?
